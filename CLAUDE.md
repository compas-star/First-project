## Vision (from the user)

- Two AI fighters (later up to 4-5). Full customization: height, weight, strength,
  speed, technique, style, powers; later a move editor and proper characters.
- From realistic street fights to demigods who fly and wreck the arena.
- **Speed mechanic (core):** body speed = own time runs faster; perception (mind)
  speed = notices attacks sooner and decides more often. Default view slows time to
  the fastest fighter's perception; "real time" mode shows the fast one as a blur.
- Physics and ragdoll are the base of the fun. Falls must be varied and interesting.
- The user rejected: a UFC-style kinematic/animation rewrite (said "забей про юфс").

## Architecture (`fighting-game/src`)

Vite + TypeScript + three.js + Rapier (`@dimforge/rapier3d-compat`, WASM inlined).
`npm run build` makes `dist/index.html` (single file) and `dist/artifact.html`.

- `sim.ts`: fight loop. World time vs fighter local time (`dl = h * bodySpeed`).
  Actions, hits (`checkStrike` geometric fist/foot contact, `resolveHit`), knockdowns,
  powers, crates. `Fighter` holds all per-fighter state.
- `physics.ts`: 11-body active ragdoll. Standing: each part steered to its world
  orientation in the target pose (external PD, own inertia: stable), scaled by
  `stability`; fallen: weak joint-relative PD (`tone`). Hover spring + walk force on
  pelvis/chest. Soft joint limits (hinge elbows/knees, cones elsewhere). IK solvers.
- `feet.ts`: planted feet with friction-like anchor, stepping, capture-point balance
  (`measureBalance`), leg IK from where the hips should be.
- `pose.ts`: target pose per moment (stance by style, strike keyframes, dazed sway,
  fall poses: fencing/fold/tuck/catch, on-floor writhing).
- `strikes.ts`: aimed punches and kicks: paths (straight/hook/upper/swing/over,
  teep/round/low/back) to the enemy's real head/body/leg via IK.
- `moves.ts`: 22 MMA strikes as data (torso keyframes + hit window + path), combo
  table `FOLLOW`, named poses `POSES`.
- `disciplines.ts`: styles (MMA, boxing, Muay Thai, kickboxing, karate, street):
  move weights, stance, range.
- `ai.ts`: `react()` reflexes (parry/counter/dash/dodge/block with perception delay),
  `think()` tactics (range, lunges, combos, feints, footwork intent).
- `brain.ts`: game plan (outbox/pressure/counter/legs/body/survive/finish) and memory
  of the opponent (what lands, how he reacts). Memory persists across fights by
  opponent name (`main.ts`, localStorage `arena-memory-v1`, faded 0.6 per fight).
- `genome.ts`, `train.ts`, `school.ts`: learned habits (~40 numbers on top of the AI:
  plan biases, eagerness, range, counters, feints, dodge/block/catch, combos, per-move
  weights). `train.ts` is a mirrored-sample evolution strategy on sparring fights,
  sliced so it runs between frames in the browser ("Тренировать против" button).
  `school.ts` holds each style's starting habits, generated by `tools/evolve.ts`.
- `powers.ts`: parry, dash, shockwave, stone skin, regeneration.
- `maps.ts` (map data + `room()` distance to boundary), `arena.ts` (map visuals: ring
  with ropes and crowd, octagon cage, alley, dojo, rooftop, void). Sim builds the map
  physics (`buildMap`), fighters keep off the edges (`footwork`).
- `skins.ts`: looks built from primitives on the rig (`dress`), 13 skins; team colour
  always shows on something. `FighterParams.skin`, default by style.
- `view.ts`: rendering, afterimages, effects, auto-framing camera, KO slow-mo zoom.
- `main.ts`: HUD, time scale ("eyes"), setup sheet, presets.
- `career.ts` + `careerUi.ts`: manager mode ("Карьера" button; the sandbox stays as is).
  Fighters with a story, stats (`bodySpeed, strength, mindSpeed, skill, evasion, cardio`)
  and hidden caps (talent). **Calendar** (user's request): the career runs by days
  (`Career.day`, day 1 = Monday 3 March; a day lasts `DAY_MS` 3 min real time, passes by
  itself in `tick`, also while closed, max 10 at once; "Следующий день" ends it now for
  1 ◆ per minute left). The day does not pass while ours has an unfought booking today
  (`waiting`) or a tournament open. Each day a fighter trains (light/normal 1 day, hard 2;
  gain applied at midnight in `endDay`) or fights; `busyUntil` = first free day; recovery
  after a fight in days. `Career.events` (`plan`, 5 weeks ahead): per league fight nights
  and tournaments on fixed cycles (`SCHEDULE`), title bout every 10 days. Ours signs up in
  advance (`book`: tour fee paid then, single fight gets its opponent; `rebook`, `unbook`,
  `challenge` = next night of that league); on the day "На бой" / "На турнир"
  (`startTour` draws the bracket, all rounds that day). Days without ours: the world fights
  (`worldDay`: 6 bouts, the title on title days, NPC tournaments `npcTour`). Screen 'cal':
  4-week grid + the chosen day's events (`evCard`); home shows the day bar and ours'
  bookings. Market refreshes every `MARKET_DAYS` (5). No `confirm()` in the career UI: the
  artifact sandbox blocks it (selling did nothing); questions use `CareerUi.asking` (Да / Нет box). Tools: `tourtest`, `worldtest`.
  Tiers Подвал → Мировой титул: single fights (purse) and 4/8-man
  tournaments (other bracket fights decided by `rating` odds). Stand-up only (`Rules.standing` turns off shots and pounces). Saved in localStorage
  `arena-career-v1`. `FighterParams.cardio` scales stamina costs and regen.
  UI is screen-based (user: the first tabbed version was unclear): home (fighter card +
  big Train / Fight buttons, how-to card once), gym (pick stat row, then intensity with
  the gain shown), fight ladder, preview (side-by-side stats, odds) before each fight,
  result screen after. Start: $300 and 100 tokens; old saves got a one-off +100 (`gifts`).
  Tournaments are real brackets (`Tour.rounds: Match[][]`, ids; 8 for the lower tiers, 16
  from the club league): screen 'bracket' lists the round's fights; ours "На бой", others
  "Смотреть" (played in the arena as AI vs AI, `settle(c, m, winner)`) or "Пропустить"
  (`settle` by `odds`), "Пропустить все чужие бои" (`settleOthers`). A loss knocks ours out,
  the rest can be watched or "Досчитать" (`closeTour`). Test buttons +◆ / ±$ in the top bar,
  "До пика" (chosen fighter: every stat = his cap).
  Living world (`Career.world`, 60 named fighters, `ensureWorld` also upgrades old saves):
  Elo ranking points (`recordBout` for every bout, ours and theirs), `rankings`/`rankOf`,
  a belt (`Career.champion`, changes only in title bouts; top-3 get a shot via `titleBout`,
  ours defends against the best contender; purse `TITLE_PURSE`). The world fights every
  calendar day (see Calendar), with trash talk (sometimes at ours); it creeps toward its caps. Single fights and tournament fields come from the world (`worldOpponent`,
  `worldField`, new faces if a league runs short). Feelings `Career.rel` (-100..100 per
  pair): KOs breed bad blood, close fights respect; <= -50 = sworn enemies: purse x1.5,
  fame x2, both fight angrier (`toParams(..., angry)`: aggression +0.25, skill x0.93).
  Screens: 'rank' (top 20 + ours, ⚔ enemies), 'profile' (any fighter, feelings, "Вызвать"
  in his league), title card on the fights screen, news on home and in the journal.
  Tool: `worldtest`.
- "Записать бой" (`recorder.ts`): the last 12 s at 15 fps (via `Sim.onStep`), body segments
  in cm as deltas + actions + hits, gzip+base64 code `БОЙ1:` (~16k chars). Button shows when
  paused (#btnRec) and on the KO card. Decode: `tools/run.sh replay code.txt out.json`, then
  `python3 tools/watch.py out.json sheet.png`.
- Punch follow-through is level (`strikes.ts`: the "through" extension has no vertical part
  except uppercuts): a hook at a man who ducks passes over him instead of tipping up into
  the air when the puncher dips (seen in a recording). A missed kick's overcommit carries
  only 25% forward and never into the other man (room-limited). Tool: `hookduck`.
- After a ground exchange, fighters standing up inside each other are eased apart so
  contact comes back (was: they fought passing through each other). Tool: `contact`.

- `labUi.ts` + `combos.ts`: combination lab ("Лаборатория связок" at the top of the setup
  sheet; the user rejected the slider strike editor: "миллион ползунков"). Tap blows in
  order (tap a step to cycle its zone), "Показать" plays it on a dummy (`Sim.lab`: striker
  obeys only, dummy stands/blocks/dodges, nothing hurts, `hurt()` logs `labHits`), slow
  motion, fixed camera angles (`view.camView`). Saved combos (localStorage
  `arena-combos-v1`, code `КОМБО1:`) are used by the AI: in `think`, for entry/bait/punish,
  50% chance to pick a saved combo whose first blow is in range (`Fighter.script` drives
  the chain in the combo flow of `runAction`). Tools: `combo1`, `combo2`.
  Built-in classics (`CLASSICS` in combos.ts, 42: boxing numbers, Dutch, Thai, karate,
  MMA, street), each tagged with styles; a fighter uses a classic only of his style and
  only if every blow has weight in his style. Pool: the player's combos first (65% when
  both fit), then classics; 50% chance to throw a combo at all. Toggle per classic
  (`arena-classics-off-v1`), "Взять" copies one into the builder. Tool: `classics`.
  A planned combination is not cut by an overcommitted miss and each next blow steps in
  as far as it needs (user: haymaker → left uppercut never started).
- Lab tab "Удар" (`strikeEditor.ts`): handles in the 3D scene, dragged with mouse/finger:
  yellow = wind-up point (`shape.chamber`), orange = arc point (`shape.via`), red ring =
  target; blue = shoulders at impact (drag x = spineTwist, y = spineLean), green = hips
  (pelvisTwist, crouch) on the impact keyframe (`impactKey`). Points are in the strike's
  body frame (`strikeFrame` in strikes.ts). A drawn `Move.shape` is taken as drawn (no
  straight-punch relative load, no target-height load). Faster/slower, reset, code
  `УДАР1:` (`moveEdits.ts`, localStorage `arena-moves-v1`). On wide screens the lab
  panel sits on the right. `window.__lab` is exposed for Playwright.

## Testing (no phone needed)

- Headless sims: `fighting-game/tools/run.sh <script> [args]` (bundles with esbuild):
  `tech "0,1,2" 4` fight stats per preset (winners, duration, hits by zone, thoughts),
  `push` push recovery, `getup` fall and rise, `aim` strike speed on a static target,
  `falls` fall types and part speeds, `plans` time spent in each plan,
  `downs` knockdowns per minute by cause, `selffall` timeline before falls nobody caused,
  `mirror` identical fighters red vs blue (side bias), `moverate` landing rate per strike,
  `kicks`/`kick1` why kicks miss, `catch` kick catches, `evolve <style> <rounds>` training.
- Visual: `node tools/strip.cjs <presetIndex> <waitMs>` in Playwright (Chromium at
  `/opt/pw-browsers`, global playwright) writes `s0..s23.png` frames; tile them with PIL
  and look. Screenshots cannot show motion; say so when reporting.
- Watching fights without a browser: `tools/run.sh watch <preset> <secs> [fps] [out.json] [seed]`
  records every frame (both ends of each part's hit segment in world space, action,
  thought, daze, wind) and events; `python3 tools/watch.py out.json sheet.png [from] [to]
  [every] [side|top|front]` draws a contact sheet of stick figures (red/blue, action under
  each, landed blows marked). Fast and readable; use it to look at what fighters do.
- Targets that worked well: equal fighters ~25-40 s fights, no runaway draws; fast
  fighters must not fall on their own; parts should not exceed ~20 m/s at body speed 1.

## Lessons learned

- Fully internal muscle torques (reaction on parent/legs) made the ragdoll explode.
  Keep external per-part PD with own inertia; make falls happen via `stability`.
- Strong braking (walk force) and strong foot anchors turn fighters into statues.
- Capture point must count a foot in the air at its landing spot, and its lead must be
  divided by body speed, otherwise single support or fast fighters look like falling.
- Damage grows with sqrt of impulse; impulse per hit is capped (~3 m/s whole-body,
  more with strength) and spread through the trunk, or bodies fly.

- A joint-limit reaction on a standing trunk tipped the pelvis over (most "fell on his
  own" falls). The reaction now fades in only as the fighter loses his feet.
- Kicks: the target was beyond the leg's length from the hip, and only the foot point
  counted. Kicker now skips in until the hip is within 0.8 leg length, shin points
  count for round/low kicks, kick balance is judged leniently. Landing rate 3% -> ~70%.
- Small personal-style differences in hand speed (6%) decided mirror fights 20:4;
  speed dominates this game, keep "handwriting" spreads tiny.
- Kicks rework (user: crooked, twisted leg, almost never land, want swung and whippy):
  new pose key `pelvisRoll` (hips tip sideways, rig Euler 'YXZ'); round kicks turn the
  hip over (roll ~-45..-55 for the rear leg) and lean the trunk away, the striking arm
  swings back; kick path 'round' = wide swing from the side with the knee pointing up and
  out (pole), foot arrives as the hit window opens (`reachEnd`), round kicks driven
  harder (KICK_DRIVE x1.4), kick-to-head aims at the jaw (`KICK_HEAD`), thigh cone 135.
  Skip-in for round kicks counts the horizontal distance from the hip (no more walking
  into the clinch). Kicks faster (bodykick 0.54, headkick 0.6). Kick catches were caught
  >50% of the time: now 0.04+0.14*tech. Tools: `kicklab` (dummy), `kickdbg`, `kickfate`
  (outcome per kick in fights). Body kick clean in fights ~25-45% (was ~8-11%).
- Feel of weight so far: hit-stop (view.hitStop), head/trunk snap (partTone dips), heavy
  legs (LIMB_MASS), kick catch + sweep.

- Condition, not hit points (user's request): `daze` (consciousness, clears with
  time and breath), `trauma` (head damage for the whole fight, weakens the chin),
  `bodyHurt` (caps wind; liver shots fold a moment later), limbs separately. One head
  blow beyond the current chin = KO; a bit less = knockdown; 3 knockdowns or taking
  clean shots while badly shaken without answering = stoppage. Unseen and turning
  blows shake more; a seen blow meets a tucked chin (technique). `hp` is now only a
  derived summary for plans and training. Tool: `ends` (how fights end).
- Head punches used to rise through the guard and chest (and the chest hit zone
  reached the chin): head hits were ~5%. Punches now load at the target's height.

- Evasion (`FighterParams.evasion`, slider "Вертлявость"): `tryDodge` in ai.ts picks
  slip/duck/weave/pull/step-back for the blow; the wrong side (`Action.into`) takes
  x1.5. Aimed strikes commit to the target once thrown (`Action.lockAim`), so dodges
  really make them miss. Defending from the end of a stagger / chaining dodges;
  a threat seen while busy stays pending. Measured: 3x more dodges, ~2x more
  dodge-counters, but no clear win-rate gain (blows in a combination land while the
  fighter reels). Highlights: `sim.moment()` -> banner + short slow-mo in main.ts.
- Lean back + counter (user: lean back with the whole body, and while he misses a high kick
  is already coming): 'отклон корпусом' head dodge (0.3 m back, `Action.lean`: trunk away,
  chin up, hips carried back), favoured vs head kicks and as range-escape vs straight shots
  (`tryDodge` scores; small random habit factor so all dodges show). With chance
  (tech, evasion, aggression, gene 'counter') the dodge gets `counterAt` (just before his
  blow passes); `Sim.counterFromDodge` fires the hardest blow that lands before he is back
  (`windupOf` vs his remaining time), head kick preferred, not if his combination goes on.
  Tool: `leanback` (~1 counter per 3-4 lean-backs; head kick counters ~30% clean).
- Less "drunk" (user: they still fight like drunks). Tool `drunk` (trunk tilt, spin, parts
  off target, time unstable/bracing, falls nobody caused, their causes); `selftrace`
  (timeline before such a fall). Fixes: (1) a kick no longer counts as falling: while
  kicking (not hit, not caught, no overcommit) stability is floored at 0.7+0.25 tech, and
  the pelvis topple check allows the intended hip roll (up.y 0.15); (2) balance counts only
  motion he did not ask for (`measureBalance(f, wanted)`: comVel minus 0.85 of his walk),
  so lunges and walking are not falls; catching steps for any error, quicker when big;
  (3) idle head movement is beats, not a sway (`pose.ts`: every ~0.5-1 s a sharp move to a
  new spot or centre, held; was a constant sinusoid up to ±30°); rock and relax smaller
  (arms 0.5, head 0.3, chest 0.15). Self-falls 1.5 -> ~0.3-0.5/min in presets 0-3; the
  remaining ones in 'Тело против ума' are body collisions with the ×4 man.
  Snappier punches: arrive at 15% of the window (was 35%), snap back with ease-out.
- Base damage 7.6 now that head shots land (fights ~50-90 s in tests).

- Finishing: an AI that sees the enemy hurt (`e.dazed`) presses; hurt fighters cover up.
- Grappling (`Sim.grapple`, state 'ground'): shoot (`shootLands`; knee meets it, sprawl
  stops it), pounce on a downed man where `disc.groundAllowed`. `groundStep` is a small
  state machine, not physics: positions guard/side/mount, top strikes (`groundHit`),
  passes, chokes/armbars from top, triangle/armbar/sweep/stand-up from bottom, escape
  rolls, referee stand-up after 14 s quiet. Fighter contact is off during it and back on
  once they are apart (`contactOff`). Skill per style in `disc.grapple` (+ technique via
  `gripOf`). Styles `wrestling`, `bjj`; presets 4 (wrestler vs boxer) and 5 (BJJ vs Thai)
  on the cage, later presets shifted by 2. Tool: `ground`. Grapplers beat strikers most
  of the time with style defaults.
- Ground look: both bodies are held in the world like standing ones, the whole body
  turned by `rootTilt` (`groundTilt`: bottom supine, feet toward the top man, slerped
  in over 0.45 s from how he lay). Anchor `Grapple.at/dir`; top kneels in guard, sits
  in mount, lies across in side control (`groundBody`, poses in `groundPose`); ground
  strikes aimed by arm IK (`groundStrikeArm`). Part speeds now mostly <13 m/s.
- Sliders "Ударка" (`striking`, scales strike damage vs the style's default) and "Борьба"
  (`grappling`, scales `gripOf` vs the style's default); missing = style default
  (`styleStriking/styleGrappling`), reset when the style changes. `groundWish` decides
  who wants the floor: strikers shoot/pounce only to finish a hurt man who is no better
  on the floor, and stand up / get up when the other is the better grappler.
  `groundWish` also counts his own game (grappling - striking), so two grapplers do go
  to the floor. Tool: `gvs <styleA> <styleB> [n]`.
  `window.__sim` is exposed for Playwright checks (e.g. force `startGrapple`).

- Alive stance (`Sim.bodyFeel`): `tension` (loose at distance, reflex-tight when close,
  threatened or busy) lowers arm/head/chest PD stiffness via `Drive.relax`, so they lag
  with their own weight; `hipYaw` trails the facing (shoulders lead a turn, pose adds the
  lag to pelvisTwist); `rock` = irregular weight drift front/back (trunk lean + a few cm
  of hip motion), `rhythm` gates the bounce on and off; breathing. The head looks at the
  enemy's head (`Drive.look`, chin tucked by technique, keeps the pose's roll).
  Tool: `pspeed` (fastest parts by state). Known: rare shin spikes ~30-38 m/s (old issue).
- Body language (`Fighter.gesture`, `Sim.gesture()`, `gesturePose` in pose.ts, own clock):
  paw (lead hand feels the range), flinch (bit on a feint), shake (hard head shot), hold
  (ribs after a body shot), nod (shrugging off a light one), beckon (after the other's
  big miss), wipe (tired, at distance). Only when no action. `guardLow/guardHigh`: elbows
  drop after body shots, gloves rise after head shots, fading. Tool: `gest`.
- Fatigue x~1.6 (user: tire faster from everything): strikes, misses, dodges, blocks,
  shots/sprawls and body hits cost more; regen is lower while tense (`tension`).
  Quick fights: wind 0.2-0.5 after a minute. Bouts: wear reaches 1 by round 5.
- Winded (`Fighter.winded`: on below 0.22 stamina, off above 0.45): backs out of range,
  no entries/answers/meets, punishes rarely, blocks (85%) instead of dodging; regen
  0.088/s (x(1-0.45 tension)). Tool: `winded` (time winded, strikes then, distance).
