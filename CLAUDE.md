## Vision (from the user)

- Two AI fighters (later up to 4-5). Full customization: height, weight, strength,
  speed, technique, style, powers; later a move editor and proper characters.
- From realistic street fights to demigods who fly and wreck the arena.
- **Speed mechanic (core):** body speed = own time runs faster; perception (mind)
  speed = notices attacks sooner and decides more often. Default view slows time to
  the fastest fighter's perception; "real time" mode shows the fast one as a blur.
- Physics and ragdoll are the base of the fun. Falls must be varied and interesting.
- The user rejected: a UFC-style kinematic/animation rewrite (said "забей про юфс").

## Architecture (`fighting-game/src`)

Vite + TypeScript + three.js + Rapier (`@dimforge/rapier3d-compat`, WASM inlined).
`npm run build` makes `dist/index.html` (single file) and `dist/artifact.html`.

- `sim.ts`: fight loop. World time vs fighter local time (`dl = h * bodySpeed`).
  Actions, hits (`checkStrike` geometric fist/foot contact, `resolveHit`), knockdowns,
  powers, crates. `Fighter` holds all per-fighter state.
- `physics.ts`: 11-body active ragdoll. Standing: each part steered to its world
  orientation in the target pose (external PD, own inertia: stable), scaled by
  `stability`; fallen: weak joint-relative PD (`tone`). Hover spring + walk force on
  pelvis/chest. Soft joint limits (hinge elbows/knees, cones elsewhere). IK solvers.
- `feet.ts`: planted feet with friction-like anchor, stepping, capture-point balance
  (`measureBalance`), leg IK from where the hips should be.
- `pose.ts`: target pose per moment (stance by style, strike keyframes, dazed sway,
  fall poses: fencing/fold/tuck/catch, on-floor writhing).
- `strikes.ts`: aimed punches and kicks: paths (straight/hook/upper/swing/over,
  teep/round/low/back) to the enemy's real head/body/leg via IK.
- `moves.ts`: 22 MMA strikes as data (torso keyframes + hit window + path), combo
  table `FOLLOW`, named poses `POSES`.
- `disciplines.ts`: styles (MMA, boxing, Muay Thai, kickboxing, karate, street):
  move weights, stance, range.
- `ai.ts`: `react()` reflexes (parry/counter/dash/dodge/block with perception delay),
  `think()` tactics (range, lunges, combos, feints, footwork intent).
- `brain.ts`: game plan (outbox/pressure/counter/legs/body/survive/finish) and memory
  of the opponent (what lands, how he reacts). Memory persists across fights by
  opponent name (`main.ts`, localStorage `arena-memory-v1`, faded 0.6 per fight).
- `genome.ts`, `train.ts`, `school.ts`: learned habits (~40 numbers on top of the AI:
  plan biases, eagerness, range, counters, feints, dodge/block/catch, combos, per-move
  weights). `train.ts` is a mirrored-sample evolution strategy on sparring fights,
  sliced so it runs between frames in the browser ("Тренировать против" button).
  `school.ts` holds each style's starting habits, generated by `tools/evolve.ts`.
- `powers.ts`: parry, dash, shockwave, stone skin, regeneration.
- `maps.ts` (map data + `room()` distance to boundary), `arena.ts` (map visuals: ring
  with ropes and crowd, octagon cage, alley, dojo, rooftop, void).
  Stylish backdrops (user's request): `skyDome` (gradient canvas dome, texture flipped for
  inside), `beam` (additive light cones, FrontSide so a camera inside sees nothing), `neonSign`,
  `fallers` (rain / petals), `flashes` (camera flashes in the crowd, more on big blows). Ring:
  dark blue canvas, hall dome with banners, beams + sweeping coloured lights. Cage: dark mat,
  lamp ring, screens. Alley: rain, wet floor, 4 more neon signs, fire drum, fire escapes, sky
  strip. Dojo: sunset over mountains, cherry petals, evening beam. Roof: dusk sky, billboard,
  water tower, moon glow. Void: nebula. Sim builds the map
  physics (`buildMap`), fighters keep off the edges (`footwork`).
- `skins.ts`: looks built from primitives on the rig (`dress`), 13 skins; team colour
  always shows on something. `FighterParams.skin`, default by style.
- `view.ts`: rendering, afterimages, effects, auto-framing camera, KO slow-mo zoom.
- `sound.ts`: all sound synthesized in WebAudio (no files), started on the first tap: blows
  (head = crack + thud, body = deep thud, block = dull pat, scaled by damage), swings
  (sim event 'swing' on every real attack), bodies landing, crunch (break/liver), bell
  (round start, three strokes at the end), crowd murmur (brown noise through two voice bands,
  ~320 Hz; was white noise = hiss, user: "super loud, sounds like hissing") whose level follows
  `view.excite` (per map `CROWD` in view.ts), "ooh" on moments, roar on the KO. Slow motion
  lowers the pitch. Levels measured offline (`window.__sound.start(OfflineAudioContext)`): crowd
  RMS ~0.008 at rest, ~0.03 at full excitement, a head hit ~0.03 RMS. Phones (user: no sound, or only
  sometimes): `Sound.wake()` on every pointerdown/pointerup/touchend/click/keydown and on
  becoming visible: resumes a suspended/'interrupted' context, plays a 1-sample buffer (old
  iOS unlock), sets `navigator.audioSession.type = 'playback'` and loops a silent <audio>
  (`SILENT_WAV`) so the iPhone silent switch does not mute Web Audio. 🔊/🔇 button in the dock (`arena-sound-v1`). Max 5 new sounds a frame.
  Also: sweat spray on clean head shots.

## Lessons learned

- Fully internal muscle torques (reaction on parent/legs) made the ragdoll explode.
  Keep external per-part PD with own inertia; make falls happen via `stability`.
- Strong braking (walk force) and strong foot anchors turn fighters into statues.
- Capture point must count a foot in the air at its landing spot, and its lead must be
  divided by body speed, otherwise single support or fast fighters look like falling.
- Damage grows with sqrt of impulse; impulse per hit is capped (~3 m/s whole-body,
  more with strength) and spread through the trunk, or bodies fly.

- A joint-limit reaction on a standing trunk tipped the pelvis over (most "fell on his
  own" falls). The reaction now fades in only as the fighter loses his feet.
- Kicks: the target was beyond the leg's length from the hip, and only the foot point
  counted. Kicker now skips in until the hip is within 0.8 leg length, shin points
  count for round/low kicks, kick balance is judged leniently. Landing rate 3% -> ~70%.
- Small personal-style differences in hand speed (6%) decided mirror fights 20:4;
  speed dominates this game, keep "handwriting" spreads tiny.
- Kicks rework (user: crooked, twisted leg, almost never land, want swung and whippy):
  new pose key `pelvisRoll` (hips tip sideways, rig Euler 'YXZ'); round kicks turn the
  hip over (roll ~-45..-55 for the rear leg) and lean the trunk away, the striking arm
  swings back; kick path 'round' = wide swing from the side with the knee pointing up and
  out (pole), foot arrives as the hit window opens (`reachEnd`), round kicks driven
  harder (KICK_DRIVE x1.4), kick-to-head aims at the jaw (`KICK_HEAD`), thigh cone 135.
  Skip-in for round kicks counts the horizontal distance from the hip (no more walking
  into the clinch). Kicks faster (bodykick 0.54, headkick 0.6). Kick catches were caught
  >50% of the time: now 0.04+0.14*tech. Tools: `kicklab` (dummy), `kickdbg`, `kickfate`
  (outcome per kick in fights). Body kick clean in fights ~25-45% (was ~8-11%).
- Feel of weight so far: hit-stop (view.hitStop), head/trunk snap (partTone dips), heavy
  legs (LIMB_MASS), kick catch + sweep.
- Recoil and pivot (user picked from a list: blows should feel heavier). `Fighter.recoil*`
  (set in `resolveHit` on a landed head/body blow, dmg > 2): on his own clock the pose throws
  the head and trunk the way the blow came (pose.ts: quick snap at 20% then back; head shots
  lean back + twist/side by the blow's x, uppercuts tip the chin up via `recoilY`; body shots
  fold), dmg > 7 knocks the rear foot back a step. Pivot (`pivotOf` in feet.ts): during a power
  blow the leg IK pole turns about the vertical (rear foot behind a rear-hand punch, lead foot
  behind a lead hook, the standing foot under a round kick) and the heel lifts; scaled by tech.
  `drunk` after: self-falls ~0.33/min (unchanged).

- Condition, not hit points (user's request): `daze` (consciousness, clears with
  time and breath), `trauma` (head damage for the whole fight, weakens the chin),
  `bodyHurt` (caps wind; liver shots fold a moment later), limbs separately. One head
  blow beyond the current chin = KO; a bit less = knockdown; 3 knockdowns or taking
  clean shots while badly shaken without answering = stoppage. Unseen and turning
  blows shake more; a seen blow meets a tucked chin (technique). `hp` is now only a
  derived summary for plans and training. Tool: `ends` (how fights end).
- Head punches used to rise through the guard and chest (and the chest hit zone
  reached the chin): head hits were ~5%. Punches now load at the target's height.

- Evasion (`FighterParams.evasion`, slider "Вертлявость"): `tryDodge` in ai.ts picks
  slip/duck/weave/pull/step-back for the blow; the wrong side (`Action.into`) takes
  x1.5. Aimed strikes commit to the target once thrown (`Action.lockAim`), so dodges
  really make them miss. Defending from the end of a stagger / chaining dodges;
  a threat seen while busy stays pending. Measured: 3x more dodges, ~2x more
  dodge-counters, but no clear win-rate gain (blows in a combination land while the
  fighter reels). Highlights: `sim.moment()` -> banner + short slow-mo in main.ts.
- Lean back + counter (user: lean back with the whole body, and while he misses a high kick
  is already coming): 'отклон корпусом' head dodge (0.3 m back, `Action.lean`: trunk away,
  chin up, hips carried back), favoured vs head kicks and as range-escape vs straight shots
  (`tryDodge` scores; small random habit factor so all dodges show). With chance
  (tech, evasion, aggression, gene 'counter') the dodge gets `counterAt` (just before his
  blow passes); `Sim.counterFromDodge` fires the hardest blow that lands before he is back
  (`windupOf` vs his remaining time), head kick preferred, not if his combination goes on.
  Tool: `leanback` (~1 counter per 3-4 lean-backs; head kick counters ~30% clean).
- Less "drunk" (user: they still fight like drunks). Tool `drunk` (trunk tilt, spin, parts
  off target, time unstable/bracing, falls nobody caused, their causes); `selftrace`
  (timeline before such a fall). Fixes: (1) a kick no longer counts as falling: while
  kicking (not hit, not caught, no overcommit) stability is floored at 0.7+0.25 tech, and
  the pelvis topple check allows the intended hip roll (up.y 0.15); (2) balance counts only
  motion he did not ask for (`measureBalance(f, wanted)`: comVel minus 0.85 of his walk),
  so lunges and walking are not falls; catching steps for any error, quicker when big;
  (3) idle head movement is beats, not a sway (`pose.ts`: every ~0.5-1 s a sharp move to a
  new spot or centre, held; was a constant sinusoid up to ±30°); rock and relax smaller
  (arms 0.5, head 0.3, chest 0.15). Self-falls 1.5 -> ~0.3-0.5/min in presets 0-3; the
  remaining ones in 'Тело против ума' are body collisions with the ×4 man.
  Snappier punches: arrive at 15% of the window (was 35%), snap back with ease-out.
- Footwork (user: "работа ног"). Tool `feetstat` (steps/s, stance crossed/narrow/wide by
  action, planted feet off their spot, catching vs planned steps via `stepStats`). Was: stance
  crossed or square 25% of the time, a rear leg left stretched far behind after lunges, ~40%
  of steps were catching steps (threshold 4 cm). Now: step and drag (`updateFeet`: the foot on
  the side of travel steps first, a foot left far behind is dragged up before the leading one
  steps again), standing errors under ~16 cm left alone, quicker steps (0.1-0.2 s), catching
  steps only past 8 cm of balance error; the leg IK aims the ankle so the sole (FOOT_LOCAL.z
  in front) lands on the anchor. Crossed 25% -> ~13%, wide ~7%. Tried and reverted: catching
  steps kept on their own side (self-falls x2-4: crossing is better than falling), stronger
  foot anchors (no effect on slip), stance lead from f.self instead of real velocity (more
  falls). Planted feet still ~5 cm off their spot even standing still (PD lag), ~25% >8 cm.
- Leg damage (user: leg kicks did nothing; should limp and slow after many, not at once).
  `Fighter.lame(leg)` = smoothstep(limbDmg / legLimit, 0.25, 0.9), `legLimit` = 1.8 x
  limbLimit; low-path kicks do x1.2 to the leg. A landed leg kick knocks the foot off its spot
  (a forced quick step along the blow) and buckles the knee (`buckleW/Side/K` on his own
  clock: crouch, hip drops that side, `pose.ts`). Lame: stride down to 50%, limp in the pose
  (lean over the good leg; hip drop and dip while the bad leg carries him), quick short steps
  off the bad leg (`updateFeet`), kicks with it rarer and slower, kicks with the other rarer
  (`weightOf`, `durOf`); a heavy kick on a leg past 0.45 lame may drop him (poise 0 ->
  'подсекли ногу'). Attackers hunt a limping leg (`moveWeight`, legs plan). HUD "хромает".
